# Research: how to find full credit card number chase app

## See your full credit card number in the Chase app
- Same flow as finding the expiration date: card > Show card number > verify > full number, expiration, and security code.
- If the option is missing or grayed out, sign out and back in or call the number on the card after identity verification (per Chase support guidance).
- Sources: CLIMB card-info walkthrough (2026), Chase support contact guidance.
- Reference frames used: shared card-details pack.
