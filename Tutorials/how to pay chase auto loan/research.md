# Research: how to pay chase auto loan

## Pay a Chase Auto loan in the app
- Path: Sign in > choose your auto account > Pay loan (or Pay Lease) > amount > Pay from > date > review > Schedule payment. First-time setup can also be done via Pay & transfer > Pay bills.
- Payments can come from a Chase account or an external account (Pay loan > Add an account). Payments can be canceled online while still pending (not in Sent / In Process / Complete status).
- Extra principal: any amount above the payment due is applied to principal.
- Due date changes: only twice over the life of the loan. Loan payoff quotes: account > More / Account services > See a payoff quote, or 1-800-336-6675.
- Sources: Chase Auto payments FAQ (chase.com/personal/auto-loans/servicing/faqs/payments) and ways-to-pay page.
- Reference frames used: loan detail built from Chase Auto servicing screens; review/confirmation shared with pay-card pack.
