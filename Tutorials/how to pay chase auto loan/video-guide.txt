In this video I’m going to show you how to how to pay chase auto loan

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Chase Auto account on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap the blue "Pay loan" button.
Step 4. Enter the payment amount (your payment due is shown on the account screen).
Step 5. Choose the account to pay from under "Pay from": your Chase checking account or an account at another bank.
Step 6. Choose the payment date, then tap "Continue".
Step 7. Review the details and tap "Schedule payment".
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows your loan payment has been scheduled.
