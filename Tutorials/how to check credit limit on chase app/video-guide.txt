In this video I'm going to show you how to how to check credit limit on chase app

Step 1. Open your chase app and sign in.
Step 2. On the home screen, tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card.
Step 3. Look at the card summary section on the account screen.
Step 4. "Credit limit" shows your total limit, here $5,000.00, with "Available credit" right above it.
Step 5. Your current balance, $418.77, shows next to your card at the top of the screen.
