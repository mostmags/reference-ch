In this video I’m going to show you how to how to pay with zelle chase

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "Pay & transfer" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Send money with Zelle".
Step 4. Tap the recipient you want to pay, or tap "Add recipient" to add a new one.
Step 5. Enter the amount, an optional message, and choose the account to pay from under "Pay from".
Step 6. Tap "Review & send".
Step 7. Check the recipient, amount, and delivery date on the review screen, then tap "Send money".
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows your money is being sent, and the recipient will get it in a few minutes.
