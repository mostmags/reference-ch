# Research: how to pay with zelle chase

## Send money with Zelle in the Chase app
- Path: Sign in > Pay & transfer > Send money with Zelle > recipient (or Add recipient) > Enter Amount > Review & send > Send money.
- Chase Zelle personal limits (typical): $2,500 per transaction / $2,500 per rolling 24 hours; recipients already registered get money in minutes; unregistered recipients must enroll first (14-day expiry).
- The Enter Amount screen shows "Today's limit for this recipient"; delivery window shown as by ~10:45 PM ET same day.
- Sources: Chase Zelle help (chase.com/personal/zelle), Zelle service limits, repo REFERENCE_INDEX zelle section (video c_R-hxWqGuc @82-91min).
