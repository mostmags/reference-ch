# Research: how to link chase bank to paypal

## Link Chase to PayPal
- PayPal Wallet > Link a card (instant with debit/credit card details) or Link a bank account (routing + account, verified by two small test deposits in 1-2 business days, or instantly via bank login where supported).
- Chase supplies the numbers; the linking happens in PayPal. Chase's security pages caution to link only through PayPal's own app/site.
- Sources: PayPal wallet help, Chase account-and-routing-numbers page.
- Reference frames used: account-numbers/show-details.png (replicated), debit card details on the show-card-number flow.
