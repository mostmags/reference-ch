In this video I’m going to show you how to how to link chase bank to paypal

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your TOTAL CHECKING account, then tap "Manage account".
Step 3. Tap "Show card number" on your debit card screen and verify with the one-time code.
Step 4. Write down the debit card number, expiration date, and security code.
Step 5. For a bank-account link, tap "Show details" and copy the routing number and account number.
Step 6. Open PayPal, go to Wallet, tap "Link a card or bank account", and enter the card details, or tap "Link a bank account" and enter the routing and account numbers.
Step 7. PayPal confirms instantly for cards; for a bank link it makes two small deposits within 1-2 business days. Enter them in PayPal to finish the link.
