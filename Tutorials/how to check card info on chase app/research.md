# Research: how to check card info on chase app

## Platform determination
Mobile-only topic, performed in the Chase Mobile app (iOS), start to finish on one platform.

## Verified flow
1. Open your chase app and sign in.  [s01.png]
2. On the home screen, tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card.  [s02.png]
3. On the account screen, tap the image of your card.  [s03.png]
4. Verify with Face ID or your passcode if prompted.  [s03.png]
5. Your full card number, expiration date, and security code appear on the card details screen.  [s04.png]
6. Use them for online purchases, or to add the card to a digital wallet.  [s04.png]

## Facts, fees, and limits
Mobile topic, credit card variant. The full number is revealed from the card image on the account screen after identity verification; digital wallets show a device account number instead of the real one. Card number rendered is fictional (4147 1120 5534 7710, matching persona ...7710).
