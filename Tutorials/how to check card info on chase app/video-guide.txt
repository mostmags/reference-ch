In this video I'm going to show you how to how to check card info on chase app

Step 1. Open your chase app and sign in.
Step 2. On the home screen, tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card.
Step 3. On the account screen, tap the image of your card.
Step 4. Verify with Face ID or your passcode if prompted.
Step 5. Your full card number, expiration date, and security code appear on the card details screen.
Step 6. Use them for online purchases, or to add the card to a digital wallet.
