In this video I’m going to show you how to how to open a chase first banking account

Step 1. Know the requirement first: Chase First Banking is for kids 6 to 17, and a parent or guardian must already have an eligible Chase checking account.
Step 2. Sign in to chase.com with the parent's profile.
Step 3. Open the Chase First Banking page under Checking and click "Open an account".
Step 4. Enter the child's details: their name, age, and your relationship to them.
Step 5. Order the child's debit card. Some branches issue it the same day.
Step 6. In the Chase app, set spending and ATM limits, choose where the card works (stores, online, ATMs), and turn on alerts.
Step 7. The child can then sign in with their own limited app access, and you can move money between your account and theirs anytime.
