# Research: how to open a chase first banking account

## Open a Chase First Banking account
- Only for existing customers: parent/guardian with an eligible Chase personal checking (Total Checking, Secure Banking, Premier Plus, College Checking). Kid ages 6-17.
- Open online via the First Banking page (existing-customer flow) or in the app; parental controls (spending/ATM limits, locations, freeze) live in the app; no monthly service fee.
- Sources: chase.com/first-banking product page, Chase First Banking help.
- Screens: rebuilt product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome (signed-in state).
