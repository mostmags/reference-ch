# Research: how to pay amazon chase credit card

## Pay the Amazon Rewards Visa (issued by Chase) in the app
- The Amazon Rewards Visa Signature card is issued and serviced by Chase, so it is paid exactly like any other Chase card in the Chase app: card > Pay card > amount > Pay from > Pay on > review > Schedule payment.
- The card appears on the Chase home screen alongside your other Chase cards; statement balance / due date come from Cardmember Services.
- Sources: Chase Amazon cardmember agreement & payment help (chase.com), WalletHub Chase credit card payment guide.
- Reference frames used: shared pay-card pack; card detail labeled Amazon Rewards Visa (...4321).
