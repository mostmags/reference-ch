# Research: how to increase chase debit card limit

## Increase debit card limits
- No in-app self-service for personal debit card limits; requests go through 1-800-935-9935 or a branch. Purchase limits are separate from ATM limits.
- Exceeding the limit declines the transaction; no fee.
- Sources: Chase banking-education ATM limits article, GOBankingRates, MyBankTracker.
- Reference frames used: shared card-management + contact pack.
