In this video I’m going to show you how to how to increase chase debit card limit

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your TOTAL CHECKING account, then tap "Manage account".
Step 3. Check the card settings: the app doesn't offer a toggle for debit card daily limits.
Step 4. Debit cards carry two limits: a daily ATM withdrawal limit and a separate daily purchase limit; both are set by Chase based on your account.
Step 5. Tap "Contact us" and call 1-800-935-9935 to ask for higher limits. They're reviewed case by case and aren't guaranteed.
Step 6. A branch visit is the other option, and teller withdrawals don't count against your ATM limit.
Step 7. For bigger purchases, split the payment or use a credit card; transactions above the limit are simply declined, with no fee.
