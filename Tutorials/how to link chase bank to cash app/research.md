# Research: how to link chase bank to cash app

## Link Chase to Cash App
- The link itself is done inside Cash App (Money tab > Link bank / Add card); Chase only supplies the card or account numbers.
- Cash App accepts debit cards for instant links (card number, expiration, CVV) or bank links via routing + account (instant verification through the card networks, or 1-2 test deposits).
- Chase routing number for Ohio: 044000037 (state-dependent; chase.com/personal/checking/account-and-routing-numbers).
- Sources: Cash App add-bank help (cash.app/help), Chase routing help.
- Reference frames used: account-numbers/show-details.png (replicated), card details built on the show-card-number flow.
