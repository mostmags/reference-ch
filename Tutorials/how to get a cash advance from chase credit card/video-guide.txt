In this video I’m going to show you how to how to get a cash advance from chase credit card

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap "Manage account".
Step 4. Under "Card setup & maintenance", tap "Manage PIN".
Step 5. On the "Get a new PIN" screen, tap "Request PIN" if you don't have one. The PIN arrives by mail in 5 to 7 business days; to set your own PIN, visit a Chase branch.
Step 6. Enter your 16-digit card number and tap "Request PIN".
Step 7. Once your PIN arrives, visit any ATM that accepts Visa: insert your credit card, enter your PIN, choose "Cash advance" or "Credit", and enter the amount.
Step 8. Expect a cash advance fee of $10 or 5% of the amount (whichever is greater), plus interest from day one. There's no grace period on cash advances.
