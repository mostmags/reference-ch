# Research: how to get a cash advance from chase credit card

## Cash advance from a Chase credit card
- Cash advances cannot be requested in the app or on chase.com; you withdraw with the physical card at a Visa/Mastercard ATM, at a branch (no PIN needed with ID), or with convenience checks.
- PIN: request through the app (Card setup & maintenance > Manage PIN) or the card's phone number; new PIN mailed in 5-7 business days; self-set PIN only at a branch.
- Fees: $10 or 5% of the transaction, whichever is greater; cash advance APR higher than purchase APR; interest accrues immediately (no grace period). Cash advance limit is typically a portion of the credit limit (often 20-30%); ATM daily caps $500-$1,000.
