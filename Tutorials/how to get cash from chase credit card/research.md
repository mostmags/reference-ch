# Research: how to get cash from chase credit card

## Get cash from a Chase credit card
- Three ways: Visa/Mastercard ATM with PIN, a Chase branch teller (ID, no PIN), or convenience checks. It cannot be requested in the app.
- Same fee/limit facts as the cash-advance topic: $10 or 5% minimum fee, immediate interest, cash-advance cap below the credit limit, ATM daily caps.
- Sources: Chase education center, firstcard.app guide (2026), thecreditpeople.com.
- Reference frames used: shared PIN pack.
