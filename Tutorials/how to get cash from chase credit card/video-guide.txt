In this video I’m going to show you how to how to get cash from chase credit card

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap "Manage account", then tap "Manage PIN" under "Card setup & maintenance".
Step 4. Request a PIN if you don't have one; it arrives by mail in 5 to 7 business days, or set your own PIN at a Chase branch.
Step 5. Enter your 16-digit card number and tap "Request PIN".
Step 6. With your PIN, go to any Visa ATM: insert your credit card, enter the PIN, choose "Cash advance", and enter the amount.
Step 7. At a Chase branch you don't need a PIN: a teller can process the cash advance with your card and photo ID.
Step 8. Fees apply: $10 or 5% of the amount (whichever is greater), interest starts immediately, and ATM owners may add their own fee.
