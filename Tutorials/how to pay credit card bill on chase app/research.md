# Research: how to pay credit card bill on chase app

## Pay a Chase credit card bill in the app
- Path: Sign in > tap the card > Pay card > amount (current/statement/other) > Pay from > Pay on > Continue > review > Schedule payment.
- Cutoff: payments made before 8:00 PM ET post the same day; payments can also be scheduled up to 14 days ahead. Payment can be made from a Chase account or a linked account at another bank.
- Sources: Chase help (chase.com/digital/customer-service/helpful-tips/credit-cards/mobile/set-up-autopay), WalletHub Chase credit card payment guide, Capital Refined manage-Chase-card-in-app walkthrough (2026).
- Reference frames used: none specific; built from Chase's documented flow and current app UI patterns (home + pay screens follow c_R-hxWqGuc home/enter-amount layouts).
