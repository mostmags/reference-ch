# Research: how to link 2 chase accounts

## Link two Chase accounts under one login
- Path: More > Profile & settings > Account settings > Manage linked accounts > Link relationship > confirm. Both profiles must share an owner (same person/business relationship).
- After linking, all linked accounts appear on the home screen and can be shown/hidden via Account settings > Show or hide accounts.
- Sources: same as business-card topic; Chase profile & settings help.
- Reference frames used: linked-accounts reference section.
