In this video I’m going to show you how to how to link 2 chase accounts

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "More" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Profile & settings".
Step 4. Tap "Account settings", then tap "Manage linked accounts".
Step 5. Under "Available relationships", find the second Chase profile you want to see here and tap "Link relationship".
Step 6. Tap "Link relationship" on the confirmation pop-up.
Step 7. The confirmation screen shows the accounts are linked, and both profiles now appear under one sign-in.
