In this video I’m going to show you how to how to open a brokerage account with chase

Step 1. Open chase.com and hover over "Investing by J.P. Morgan".
Step 2. Open the J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing page and click "Open an account".
Step 3. Sign in with your Chase profile, or create one when prompted.
Step 4. Fill in the application: personal details, employment, and a short investment profile (goals, experience, risk tolerance).
Step 5. Choose the account type (individual, joint, or retirement) and submit.
Step 6. Fund the account: transfers from a Chase account are usually available in minutes, from an outside bank in 1 to 3 business days.
Step 7. You're done: commission-free online stock and ETF trades, with no account minimums.
