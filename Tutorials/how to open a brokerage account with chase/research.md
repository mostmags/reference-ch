# Research: how to open a brokerage account with chase

## Open a J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing account
- chase.com > Investing by J.P. Morgan > Self-Directed Investing > Open an account; ~10 minutes; personal + employment + investment profile.
- No account minimums, no commissions for online stock/ETF trades; fund from Chase (minutes) or linked external bank (1-3 business days).
- Sources: chase.com investing product pages, J.P. Morgan investing FAQ.
- Screens: rebuilt product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome.
