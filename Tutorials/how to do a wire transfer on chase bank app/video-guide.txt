In this video I’m going to show you how to how to do a wire transfer on chase bank app

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "Pay & transfer" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Wire money".
Step 4. Tap "Get started" on the intro screen.
Step 5. Enter the recipient's bank routing number under "Add recipient bank details" and tap "Continue" when the bank name is confirmed.
Step 6. Enter the recipient's account number, name, and address under "Add recipient details", then tap "Continue".
Step 7. Choose a payment purpose, review the wire amount, the $25.00 wire fee, and the total on the "Does everything look OK?" screen, then tap "Schedule wire".
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows your wire has been scheduled.
