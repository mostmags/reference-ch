# Research: how to do a wire transfer on chase bank app

## Wire transfer on the Chase app
- Path: Sign in > Pay & transfer > Wire money > Get started > Step 1 of 3 bank details > Step 2 of 3 recipient details > Step 3 of 3 review > Schedule wire.
- Domestic wire fee: $25.00 per wire (checking accounts). Incoming wires: $15.
- Same-day domestic wires must be submitted before 4:00 PM ET; wires can be scheduled 24/7 and dated up to 30 days in advance.
- Chase daily online wire limit: $25,000 (shown on the scheduling screen); amounts above require a branch.
- Chase's scam warning screen appears before scheduling (never wire to someone you haven't verified; Chase may call to verify first-time wires).
