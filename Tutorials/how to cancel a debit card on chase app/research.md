# Research: how to cancel a debit card on chase app

## Platform determination
Mobile-only topic, performed in the Chase Mobile app (iOS), start to finish on one platform.

## Verified flow
1. Open your chase app and sign in.  [s01.png]
2. Tap your TOTAL CHECKING account, the checking account your debit card is tied to.  [s02.png]
3. Tap "Manage account" to open your account tools.  [s03.png]
4. Tap "Replace a lost or damaged card". In the app, canceling a card is done through replacement: Chase immediately cancels your old card number and issues a new one.  [s04.png]
5. Choose "My card is lost" as the reason, or the one that fits, then tap "Next".  [s05.png]
6. Check your mailing address, keep "Regular delivery", and tap "Next".  [s06.png]
7. Review the "Does everything look OK?" screen and tap "Replace card" to cancel your old card.  [s07.png]
8. Your replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days. To cancel without a replacement, call the number on the back of your card.  [s07.png]

## Facts, fees, and limits
Negative claim, verified: the Chase Mobile app has no direct cancel/close-card button. The in-app route that cancels the card number is Replace a lost or damaged card, which immediately cancels the old number and ships a new one; Lock card is the temporary option. A permanent cancel without replacement requires calling Chase (1-800-935-9935) or a branch. Replacement shipping shown as Regular delivery, 5 to 7 business days, no charge.
