In this video I'm going to show you how to how to cancel a debit card on chase app

Step 1. Open your chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your TOTAL CHECKING account, the checking account your debit card is tied to.
Step 3. Tap "Manage account" to open your account tools.
Step 4. Tap "Replace a lost or damaged card". In the app, canceling a card is done through replacement: Chase immediately cancels your old card number and issues a new one.
Step 5. Choose "My card is lost" as the reason, or the one that fits, then tap "Next".
Step 6. Check your mailing address, keep "Regular delivery", and tap "Next".
Step 7. Review the "Does everything look OK?" screen and tap "Replace card" to cancel your old card.
Step 8. Your replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days. To cancel without a replacement, call the number on the back of your card.
