In this video I’m going to show you how to how to link chase business credit card to personal account

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "More" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Profile & settings".
Step 4. Tap "Account settings", then tap "Manage linked accounts".
Step 5. Under "Available relationships", find the business profile that holds your business credit card and tap "Link relationship".
Step 6. Tap "Link relationship" on the confirmation pop-up.
Step 7. The green confirmation screen shows your account has been successfully linked. The business card now appears in this app alongside your personal accounts.
