# Research: how to link chase business credit card to personal account

## Link a Chase business credit card to the personal app login
- Path: More > Profile & settings > Account settings > Manage linked accounts > Available relationships > Link relationship > confirm.
- Works when both profiles share an owner; business cards held under a separate EIN-only profile need authorization on that profile instead.
- Source video for reference frames: "How To Add Chase Business Card To Chase App (Full Guide)" (5RZjYNDom5k), frames in repo linked-accounts/ section.
