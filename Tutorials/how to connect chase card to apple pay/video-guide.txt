In this video I’m going to show you how to how to connect chase card to apple pay

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap "Manage account".
Step 4. Under "Card setup & maintenance", tap "Add card to digital wallets".
Step 5. Tap "Apple Pay".
Step 6. On the "Add to Wallet" screen, check the card details and tap "Add to Apple Wallet".
Step 7. Keep the screen open while Chase verifies your card. You may be asked for a one-time code.
Step 8. The "Your card is ready to use" confirmation shows the card was added to Apple Pay. Hold your iPhone near a contactless reader to pay.
