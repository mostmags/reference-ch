# Research: how to connect chase card to apple pay

## Add a Chase card to Apple Pay
- Path: Sign in > tap card > Manage account > Card setup & maintenance > Add card to digital wallets > Apple Pay > Add to Apple Wallet > verification > ready.
- Any eligible Chase credit, debit, or Chase Liquid card can be added; you can add to Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal from the app (Chase digital wallet help page).
- Verification: Chase may send a one-time code or auto-verify; a confirmation email follows. Device limits apply (roughly 8-12 devices per card).
- The first card added becomes the default in Apple Pay; change under Settings > Wallet & Apple Pay.
- Sources: chase.com/digital/customer-service/helpful-tips/online-banking/mobile/digital-wallet, chase.com Apple Pay FAQs, Capital Refined "How to add your Chase card to Apple Pay" (2026), Travel with Grant walkthrough.
