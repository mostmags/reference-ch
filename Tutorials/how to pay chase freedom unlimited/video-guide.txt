In this video I’m going to show you how to how to pay chase freedom unlimited

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Freedom Unlimited card on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap the blue "Pay card" button.
Step 4. Choose how much to pay: current balance, statement balance, or another amount.
Step 5. Choose the account to pay from under "Pay from".
Step 6. Choose the payment date under "Pay on", then tap "Continue".
Step 7. Review the details and tap "Schedule payment".
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows your Freedom Unlimited payment has been scheduled.
