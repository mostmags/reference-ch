# Research: how to pay chase freedom unlimited

## Pay a Chase Freedom Unlimited card in the app
- Same flow as any Chase credit card payment: card screen > Pay card > amount > Pay from > Pay on > review > Schedule payment.
- Autopay option: Manage account > Autopay; choose statement balance to always pay in full.
- Sources: Chase Freedom Unlimited payment help (chase.com/digital/customer-service/helpful-tips/credit-cards/mobile/set-up-autopay), Capital Refined walkthrough (2026).
- Reference frames used: same pack as pay-credit-card-bill topic (shared screens).
