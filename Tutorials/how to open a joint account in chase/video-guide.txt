In this video I’m going to show you how to how to open a joint account in chase

Step 1. Open chase.com and hover over "Checking".
Step 2. Open the Chase Total Checking page and click "Open an account".
Step 3. Choose "Joint account" when the application asks for the ownership type.
Step 4. Enter the first owner's details, then the second owner's: name, date of birth, Social Security number, and address.
Step 5. Pick the funding amount and source, and review the account terms together.
Step 6. Submit: both owners get account access in the app, and each can order a debit card.
Step 7. Fund the account to finish setup, and set up direct deposit to skip the monthly service fee.
