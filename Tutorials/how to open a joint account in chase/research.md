# Research: how to open a joint account in chase

## Open a joint Chase checking account
- chase.com > Checking > Total Checking > Open an account; joint ownership selected in the application; both owners supply identity details; both get app access and debit cards.
- Waive the monthly fee with qualifying activities (direct deposit, balance, linked accounts).
- Sources: chase.com checking product pages, Chase checking service-fee schedule.
- Screens: rebuilt product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome.
