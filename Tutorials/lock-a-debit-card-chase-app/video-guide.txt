In this video I'm going to show you how to lock a debit card in the chase app

Step 1. Open your chase app and sign in.
Step 2. On the home screen, tap your TOTAL CHECKING account, the checking account your debit card is tied to.
Step 3. Scroll down and tap "Manage account" to open your account tools.
Step 4. At the top of the Manage account screen, tap "Lock & unlock card".
Step 5. Read the "Misplaced your card?" info. Locking blocks new purchases and ATM withdrawals, but it won't block fees, credits, digital wallet purchases or repeating transactions.
Step 6. Next to your DEBIT/ATM CARD, tap the blue "Lock card" button.
Step 7. The green "You've locked your card." message confirms your card is locked, and the button now reads "Unlock card".
Step 8. When you find your card, come back to this screen and tap "Unlock card" to use it again.
