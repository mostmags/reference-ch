# Research: how to pay chase credit card from another bank

## Pay a Chase credit card from a bank at another institution
- Same Pay card flow; under Pay from, select an external account or tap Add an account. Adding an external account needs the other bank's routing + account numbers and can take 1-2 business days to verify (small test deposits / instant verification).
- External payments made before 8:00 PM ET on a business day post the same day; allow extra days for first payments from a newly linked account.
- Sources: Chase pay-credit-card help (chase.com/personal/credit-cards/manage/pay-credit-card), Chase auto-loans ways-to-pay page (same external-account mechanic), repo external-accounts reference section.
- Reference frames used: external-accounts/how-to-add.png (layout), pay card pack screens.
