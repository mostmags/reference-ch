In this video I’m going to show you how to how to pay chase credit card from another bank

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your Freedom Unlimited credit card on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap the blue "Pay card" button.
Step 4. Choose how much to pay: current balance, statement balance, or another amount.
Step 5. Under "Pay from", choose your account at another bank. If it isn't listed, tap "Add an account" and link your bank using its routing and account numbers.
Step 6. Choose the payment date under "Pay on", then tap "Continue".
Step 7. Review the details and tap "Schedule payment".
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows your payment has been scheduled.
