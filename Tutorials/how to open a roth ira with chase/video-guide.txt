In this video I’m going to show you how to how to open a roth ira with chase

Step 1. Open chase.com and go to "Investing by J.P. Morgan".
Step 2. Open the retirement section and choose Roth IRA, then click "Open an account".
Step 3. Sign in with your Chase profile and fill in the application: identity, employment, and the investment profile questions.
Step 4. Confirm you have earned income this year (a Roth IRA requires it under IRS rules) and choose your contribution amount up to the annual limit.
Step 5. Pick your investments: your own stocks and ETFs, or a target portfolio.
Step 6. Fund the Roth IRA from a Chase or linked account.
Step 7. You're done: qualified withdrawals in retirement are tax-free. Set up automatic contributions in the app to keep it growing.
