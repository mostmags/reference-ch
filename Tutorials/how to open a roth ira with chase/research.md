# Research: how to open a roth ira with chase

## Open a Roth IRA with Chase
- Opened under J.P. Morgan Self-Directed Investing (retirement account type) on chase.com; requires earned income (IRS); contributions follow the annual IRS limit (income phases apply).
- Investments: self-directed or managed portfolios; funding from Chase (minutes) or external bank (1-3 days).
- Sources: chase.com retirement/IRA pages, J.P. Morgan IRA FAQ.
- Screens: rebuilt product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome.
