In this video I’m going to show you how to how to set up direct deposit chase app

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your TOTAL CHECKING account on the home screen.
Step 3. Tap "Show details" to display your account and routing numbers.
Step 4. Tap "Transfer" (or More) and choose "Set up direct deposit" to open the Direct Deposit screen.
Step 5. Give your employer the routing number and account number shown, or tap "Copy" next to each one.
Step 6. If your employer needs a form, tap "Direct Deposit form (PDF)" to download the prefilled form and send it to your employer's payroll department.
