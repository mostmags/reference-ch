# Research: how to set up direct deposit chase app

## Set up direct deposit (Chase app)
- Path: Sign in > tap TOTAL CHECKING > Show details (routing + account numbers) > Direct Deposit screen > share numbers with employer, or download prefilled PDF.
- Chase routing number for Ohio (persona's address, Columbus OH): 044000037. Chase routing numbers vary by state (full list: chase.com/personal/checking/account-and-routing-numbers).
- The prefilled form (PDF) includes name, address, account number, routing number, VOID check graphic, and authorization language.
- Government benefits (Social Security, military pay) go through GoDirect.gov instead.
- Sources: Chase direct deposit help (chase.com/personal/checking/direct-deposit), repo REFERENCE_INDEX direct-deposit + account-numbers sections.
