# Research: how to delete a payee on chase app

## Delete a payee on the Chase app
- Path: Sign in > Pay & transfer > Pay bills > tap payee > Delete payee > confirm.
- Deleting a payee does not cancel payments already scheduled; cancel scheduled payments separately before deleting the payee.
- Bill Pay cutoff: payments scheduled before 8:00 PM ET are credited the same day.
- Sources: Chase Bill Pay help (chase.com/digital/customer-service/helpful-tips), GetHuman Chase payee-deletion guide (gethuman.com), r/Chase manage pay-from accounts thread.
