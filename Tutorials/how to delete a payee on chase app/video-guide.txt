In this video I’m going to show you how to how to delete a payee on chase app

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "Pay & transfer" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Pay bills".
Step 4. Find the payee you want to remove in your payee list and tap it.
Step 5. On the payee detail screen, tap "Delete payee".
Step 6. Tap "Delete" on the confirmation pop-up to confirm.
Step 7. The confirmation screen shows the payee has been removed from your bill pay list.
