In this video I'm going to show you how to how to check chase credit card application status

Step 1. Go to chase.com and click "Sign in" at the top right.
Step 2. Enter your username and password, then click "Sign in".
Step 3. On your accounts page, click the menu icon in the top left.
Step 4. Scroll down the menu and click "Application status".
Step 5. The application status page shows your card, the date you applied, and the current status, here "In review".
Step 6. Most decisions arrive within 7 to 10 business days. For an update sooner, call Chase at 1-888-338-2586.
