# Research: how to check chase credit card application status

## Platform determination
Desktop-only topic, performed on chase.com in a browser; the app is not used.

## Verified flow
1. Go to chase.com and click "Sign in" at the top right.  [s01.png]
2. Enter your username and password, then click "Sign in".  [s02.png]
3. On your accounts page, click the menu icon in the top left.  [s03.png]
4. Scroll down the menu and click "Application status".  [s04.png]
5. The application status page shows your card, the date you applied, and the current status, here "In review".  [s05.png]
6. Most decisions arrive within 7 to 10 business days. For an update sooner, call Chase at 1-888-338-2586.  [s05.png]

## Facts, fees, and limits
Desktop-only topic. Application status is checked on chase.com after signing in (menu icon, upper left, Application Status). Chase has no standalone public status tracker; the phone line 1-888-338-2586 is the alternative. Checking status costs nothing and triggers no new credit inquiry.
