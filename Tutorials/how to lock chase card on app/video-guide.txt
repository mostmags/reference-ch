In this video I’m going to show you how to how to lock chase card on app

Step 1. Open your chase app and tap on your TOTAL CHECKING account.
Step 2. If the account-tools message appears, tap “Got it” to dismiss it.
Step 3. Tap “Manage account” to open your account tools.
Step 4. Under the account tools grid, tap “Lock & unlock card”.
Step 5. Tap the toggle next to “Lock card” to lock it.
Step 6. Keep the screen open while the lock processes.
Step 7. The “You have locked your card” confirmation shows the card is locked. New purchases will be declined.
Step 8. To unlock it, come back to the same screen and tap the toggle again. Locking doesn't block fees, credits, digital wallet payments, or recurring transactions.
