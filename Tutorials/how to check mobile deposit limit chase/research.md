# Research: how to check mobile deposit limit chase

## Platform determination
Mobile-only topic, performed in the Chase Mobile app (iOS), start to finish on one platform.

## Verified flow
1. Open your chase app and sign in.  [s01.png]
2. On the home screen, tap "Deposit checks" in the quick actions row.  [s02.png]
3. If the Chase QuickDeposit agreement appears, tick the box to agree, then tap "Deposit a check".  [s03.png]
4. On the QuickDeposit screen, tap "Deposit to" and select your TOTAL CHECKING account.  [s04.png]
5. Right below the deposit amount field, "Your current mobile deposit limit is $7,500.00" shows your limit.  [s04.png]
6. Tap "Learn more" for details on deposit limits and when your money is available.  [s04.png]

## Facts, fees, and limits
Chase does not publish universal deposit limits; the limit is shown in the app on the QuickDeposit screen once you choose the account (daily and 30-day). Limits vary by account history; the widely cited personal baseline is $2,000/day and $5,000/30-day, and the QuickDeposit agreement caps a single deposit at $25,000. ATM and teller deposits have no dollar cap. No fee for checking your limit.
