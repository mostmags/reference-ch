# Research: how to get chase card number before it arrives

## Use a Chase card before the physical card arrives
- Honest negative: Chase does not display the full card number in the app before the card arrives; the "Show card number" option populates only once the card is active/arrived.
- The immediate path is a digital wallet: right after approval, the card can be added to Apple Pay/Google Pay from the app and used for contactless and in-app purchases (Chase offers instant wallet provisioning on approval for most cards).
- Sources: Chase digital wallet help, Capital Refined Apple Pay walkthrough (2026), CLIMB card-info guide (2026).
- Reference frames used: shared digital-wallet pack.
