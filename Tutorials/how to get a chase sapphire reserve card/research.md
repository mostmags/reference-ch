# Research: how to get a chase sapphire reserve card

## Apply for Chase Sapphire Reserve
- Application runs on chase.com (desktop or mobile web) or in the app under Credit cards > Apply; no online-banking enrollment required to apply.
- Decision in about 60 seconds for most; pending cases reviewed by email/mail. Approved cards arrive in 5-7 business days; instant digital wallet provisioning offered at approval.
- Approval factors: credit score, income, and Chase's 5/24 rule (no new cards if you opened 5+ cards in 24 months across issuers).
- Sources: chase.com Sapphire Reserve product page, Chase application FAQ, NerdWallet Sapphire Reserve review (2026).
- Screens: rebuilt chase.com product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome.
