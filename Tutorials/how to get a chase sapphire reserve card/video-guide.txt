In this video I’m going to show you how to how to get a chase sapphire reserve card

Step 1. Open chase.com in your browser.
Step 2. Hover over "Credit cards" and open the Chase Sapphire Reserve product page.
Step 3. Review the highlights, then click "Apply now".
Step 4. Fill in the application: your legal name, home address, date of birth, Social Security number, and annual income. You don't need an online banking profile to apply.
Step 5. Review the terms (annual fee, APR) and submit the application.
Step 6. Most decisions take about 60 seconds; some applications need more review and Chase emails you the result.
Step 7. If approved, the card arrives in 5 to 7 business days, and you can add it to Apple Pay or Google Pay right away from the approval page.
Step 8. Activate the card in the app or by phone when it arrives.
