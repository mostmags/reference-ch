In this video I’m going to show you how to how to delete zelle recipient on chase app

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap "Pay & transfer" on the bottom navigation bar.
Step 3. Tap "Send money with Zelle".
Step 4. Tap the gear icon in the top right, then tap "Manage recipients".
Step 5. Find the recipient you want to remove and tap their name.
Step 6. Tap "Delete recipient".
Step 7. Tap "Delete" on the confirmation pop-up to confirm.
Step 8. The confirmation screen shows the recipient has been removed from your Zelle recipients.
