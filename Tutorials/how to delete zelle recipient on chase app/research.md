# Research: how to delete zelle recipient on chase app

## Delete a Zelle recipient on the Chase app
- Path: Sign in > Pay & transfer > Send money with Zelle > gear > Manage recipients > tap recipient > Delete recipient > confirm.
- Deleting a recipient does not cancel payments already sent or scheduled.
- Chase's Zelle send limit (typical): $2,500 per transaction / $2,500 per day for personal accounts; today's limit per recipient is shown on the Enter Amount screen.
- Sources: Chase Zelle help (chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/credit-intelligence/how-to-use-zelle), Zelle consumer protection info, repo REFERENCE_INDEX zelle section (video c_R-hxWqGuc @82-91min).
