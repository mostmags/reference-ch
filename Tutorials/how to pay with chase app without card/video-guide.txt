In this video I’m going to show you how to how to pay with chase app without card

Step 1. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 2. Tap your card, then tap "Manage account".
Step 3. Under "Card setup & maintenance", tap "Add card to digital wallets".
Step 4. Tap "Apple Pay" (or Google Pay), then tap "Add to Apple Wallet" and complete verification.
Step 5. The "Your card is ready to use" screen confirms your card is in your phone's wallet.
Step 6. To pay in a store, wake your iPhone and hold it near the contactless reader until "Done" and the checkmark appear. No physical card needed.
Step 7. For bills and friends, use "Pay & transfer" in the app: Pay bills, Zelle, and transfers all work without your physical card.
