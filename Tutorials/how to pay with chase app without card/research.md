# Research: how to pay with chase app without card

## Pay with the Chase app without the physical card
- In stores: add the card to Apple Pay/Google Pay from the app, then tap to pay at contactless readers (Chase tap-to-pay).
- In app: Pay bills, Zelle, transfers, and mobile check deposit all run from the phone without the physical card.
- Sources: chase.com digital wallet + tap-to-pay pages, Zelle help.
- Reference frames used: shared digital-wallet pack.
