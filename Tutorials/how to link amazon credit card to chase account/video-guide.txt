In this video I’m going to show you how to how to link amazon credit card to chase account

Step 1. Know this first: the Amazon Rewards Visa is issued by Chase, so once you're approved it appears in the Chase app automatically under "Credit cards". There is no separate linking step.
Step 2. Open the Chase app and sign in.
Step 3. If the card doesn't show on your home screen, tap "More", then "Profile & settings".
Step 4. Tap "Account settings", then tap "Show or hide accounts".
Step 5. Find "Amazon Rewards Visa" and tap the toggle to show it.
Step 6. Go back to the home screen: your Amazon Rewards Visa now appears with your other Chase cards, ready to pay and manage.
