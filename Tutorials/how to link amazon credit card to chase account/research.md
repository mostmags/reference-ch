# Research: how to link amazon credit card to chase account

## Link (unhide) the Amazon Rewards Visa in the Chase app
- Honest negative: the Amazon Rewards Visa Signature is issued and serviced by Chase, so it appears automatically once approved; nothing needs linking.
- If hidden: More > Profile & settings > Account settings > Show or hide accounts > toggle the card on.
- Sources: Chase Amazon Visa card page, Chase profile & settings help, WalletHub Amazon-card servicing notes.
- Reference frames used: linked-accounts/profile-account-settings.png (replicated), home screen shared pack.
