# Research: how to open a high yield savings account chase

## Open a Chase savings account (honest framing)
- No Chase personal product is branded "high yield savings"; personal options are Chase Savings and Premier Savings (rate tiers by balance). Compare against online banks if rate is the goal.
- Fee waivers: $0 opening with Autosave (or $300+ daily balance / linked Premier Plus checking); Autosave round-ups + recurring transfers in app.
- Sources: chase.com savings product + rates pages, Chase savings service-fee schedule.
- Screens: rebuilt product/application/confirmation pages on the live-capture chrome.
