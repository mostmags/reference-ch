In this video I’m going to show you how to how to open a high yield savings account chase

Step 1. Know this first: Chase doesn't market a personal account under the name "high yield savings". Its savings options are Chase Savings and Premier Savings; rates run below online-only banks, so check the current rates page before you open one.
Step 2. Open chase.com and hover over "Savings & CDs".
Step 3. Open the Chase Savings page and click "Open an account".
Step 4. Sign in or continue as a new customer, and fill in the application (identity, address, funding source).
Step 5. Skip the monthly service fee by opening with $0 when you set up an automatic savings plan, or meet a balance requirement.
Step 6. Turn on Autosave in the app: round-ups on debit purchases and a recurring transfer.
Step 7. Fund the account from checking or an outside bank, and you're set.
